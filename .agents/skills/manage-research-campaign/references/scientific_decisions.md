# BR scientific decisions within a formal campaign

During a research episode, actively use BR scientific capabilities when they
can resolve a question that affects the hypothesis, test, or interpretation.
Mock validation, debugging, and status-only work do not trigger this route.
Reuse current-session capability information; inspect an exposed, allowed tool's
schema only as needed. A missing capability in a limited profile is an
integration gap, not a reason to install tools, retry discovery, audit the
server, or substitute another profile.

- Before selecting or freezing explanations, use source-backed KG or literature
  evidence to challenge assumptions and the strongest alternative. Reuse the
  applicable ideation/prelude evidence. Preserve the V3/V4 outcome-blind
  selection contract and the frozen V5/V6 prelude, lineage, and no-fallback
  rules; never reconstruct or rewrite selection after target outcomes.
- When a consequential design choice remains unresolved, seek focused domain
  critique through an exposed, allowed plan-review surface, such as
  `pipeline_plan_review`. Ask about the actual uncertainty in the estimand,
  independent unit, controls, split, or null model; schema validation alone is
  not scientific critique. Any resulting change stays within the current
  authorization and frozen experiment contract or returns to the scientist.
- After a substantive authorized result, use the matching scientific-review
  surface, such as `request_scientific_review` or an applicable run/autoresearch
  review, before settling its interpretation or successor question. Reuse an
  applicable completed review. Send only evidence authorized for that surface;
  closed outcomes and restricted artifacts never go to a public MCP endpoint.

Record the question, evidence/tool reference, and decision impact, including
`no change`, in the existing `outputs/experiment_log.md`; add no ledger, schema,
or call quota. Runner-owned workers hand review questions and artifact references
to their coordinator; delivery, review, Society, and confirmation ownership
remain unchanged. Early scientific critique is advisory. Formal Society still
uses its frozen packet and human gates, and neither route grants execution,
reward, scientific acceptance, or a Landscape transition.
