# Autoresearch-depth audit

## Scope

EP01--EP17 except EP18, plus EP19--EP20, are 19 direct adaptive-search
episodes. The two old NARPS records are immutable priors with non-episode IDs.
EP18 is the sole incomplete local draft and does not enter the depth table
below. This audit asks whether each current complete contract can measure
sustained autoresearch depth rather than a one-shot fixed comparison.

The design-depth requirement is satisfied at the contract level. Realized
evidence remains zero: no current episode has completed the required adaptive
lineage, configuration lock, and one-shot audit.

This zero is an evidence statement, not a launch-readiness verdict. Local
episode execution may begin by explicit scientist invocation; bootstrap,
validation, adaptive lineage, configuration lock, and held-out evaluation are
then realized inside the episode. `EPISODE_READINESS.yaml` is optional
canonical-confirmation metadata and does not gate the local launcher.

EP02 now has a full bounded search contract, but none of its depth has been
realized. Its materializer passed integrity checks while remaining unsealed,
and generic calibration v2 returned `selected_rule: null` (0 of 216 eligible).
Consequently the policy authorizes no primary audit opening or positive
mechanistic terminal until its confidentiality, endpoint, assignment,
endpoint-specific calibration, signoff, evaluator, and canonical blockers are
resolved.

## Depth rubric

Every episode must have:

1. a development region where outcomes may guide admissible successors;
2. a frozen but nontrivial hypothesis/operator grammar;
3. mandatory branch coverage before qualified patience;
4. at least two outcome-linked successor cycles and two
   incumbent/challenger decisions;
5. an append-only, hash-chained trial ledger;
6. at least 40% post-coverage falsification, ablation, influence, alternative,
   or replication work;
7. numeric trial and compute ceilings with a nonterminal incumbent; and
8. one configuration lock followed by the episode's declared held-out
   evaluation opened at most once. Permission separation is required only when
   the episode claims that stronger boundary.

## Interpretation

AI proposal generation may continue for a long time, but a finite development
set cannot provide independent information forever. Each episode therefore has
a finite scientific round. Continued search after an audit requires a new
episode and a genuinely new audit source; it is not another numbered local
revision of the same evidence.

The fail-closed machine-readable gate is
`ADAPTIVE_EVIDENCE_POLICY.yaml`. Passing its structural requirements measures
search depth, not scientific truth or canonical acceptance.
