# EP01–EP21 design review — 2026-10-02

This completes a scope, closest-literature and clarity review of all 21 current
episodes. Each linked episode note records checked primary sources, the
candidate contribution, repairs and remaining empirical uncertainty. Reviews
were parallel; edits were episode-scoped and sequential.

The current questions are substantially more coherent after the repairs.
This is not a verdict that 21 standalone papers are ready: some episodes are
supporting components, technical references, benchmarks or adapted reproduction.
A focused literature comparison does not establish exhaustive global priority.

- **EP03:** fixed whole-prediction polarity. Fit in methods-defined canonical
  coordinates, then restore the sign to both the family comparator and the
  complete residual model. The baseline does not cancel; reversal is an
  implementation invariant, not semantic evidence.
- **EP06:** separated numerical comparability from candidate-specific sign
  eligibility. A measurable unsupported rival no longer vetoes another
  supported component; an unscoreable rival is not silently removed.
- **EP17:** replaced the training-single-property admission requirement with
  claim-specific eligibility for fixed-representation measurement comparisons.
  Recipe differences still prohibit causal training claims. Also exposed both
  the fixed-weight forecast identity and the beta OLS intercept identity.
  Beta's block-mean forecast companion is now explicitly separate from its
  pooled-image primary edge.
- **EP07/09/18/19:** repaired interpretations that promoted non-detection,
  equivalence or fitted ablation into an identified biological explanation.
- **EP08:** distinguished sensor-dependent trial value from aggregate
  factorial policy interaction, without restricting open exploration.
- **EP15/20:** reconciled narrative conclusions with their actual comparison
  rules: multiple adequate accounts are not a simplicity-based biological
  winner; a virtual design that fails its required rule cannot advance.
- **EP04:** foregrounded the existing within-category OOD native-geometry
  comparison, preserving direct VLM–fMRI alignment rather than adding
  reproduction or a learned primary mapping.
- **EP01/02/10–14/16/21:** clarified prior-art overlap and contribution type,
  preserving strong existing questions where another rewrite would add little.
  EP21's paper narrative now reflects the latest recorded, still-sealed
  provisioning and separately bounded source-only preparation.

EP03, EP06 and EP17 contain explicit prospective design-definition amendments.
Other changes principally align writing with existing comparisons and claim
limits. No active experimental endpoint or runtime contract in EP10–12 or
EP21 was changed.

## Keep the scientific jobs distinct

The LFP family is not four independent repetitions of “LFP predicts neural
activity.” EP05 targets unusual individual reaches; EP07 targets information
added by earlier days; EP06 supplies its recording-domain component analysis;
EP08 targets conditional acquisition value. The existing EP06–EP07 manuscript
integration does not merge their data roles or turn reused recordings into
fresh replication.

The anatomy family also needs distinct deliverables. EP09 asks about residual
own-dendrite/exact-target information; EP10 about terminal implementation inside
a common target; EP11 about reusable allocation descriptions beyond a continuum;
EP12 about structural errors introduced by type averaging. Another cluster,
additional controls, or a different atlas label alone does not earn these claims.

EP04 compares native image/brain geometry under stimulus shift; EP17 studies
measurement dependence of fixed model comparisons; EP18 tests how concept
grouping shares predictive EEG information; EP21 reproduces neural-code
conversion. Their related stimulus/model ecosystems do not make them
independent demonstrations of one universal alignment phenomenon.

## Where more prose cannot settle the question

- **EP10 and EP12:** biological novelty remains dependent on the actual named
  pathway/circuit. Public anatomy motivates candidates but cannot certify an
  unchosen case. EP12's DNg02 illustration is not an adopted endpoint or a
  license to pool finer annotations.
- **EP11:** an informative answer requires the existing recovery/precision
  work and defensible biological replicate support. Better writing cannot
  create either.
- **EP16:** recovery-estimand aggregation and literal chronological exposure
  remain explicit pre-execution scientific choices; no numerical defaults were
  invented.
- **EP02, EP04 and EP17:** their bounded reanalysis/benchmark/methods value is
  clearer than a claim to an assured independent mechanistic discovery.
- **EP13:** retain the useful classical estimability reference and certificates;
  a stronger standalone research contribution would require a genuinely
  different question, not inflated wording.
- **EP21:** judge adapted reproduction on its evidence and adaptation limits.
  New-method novelty is not its purpose.
- **All empirical episodes:** effect size, precision, transfer and the specific
  unanticipated scientific pattern remain to be learned. A positive score by
  itself is not sufficient; a precise negative answer can still be useful.

Open exploration remains open. This review adds no compulsory pilot, fixed
small hypothesis menu, speculative circuit shortlist or new experiment quota.
