# Research landscape

This is a local reading map, not the canonical Brain Researcher Landscape.
Episode-specific numbers and claims remain inside their episodes.

## Current themes

### Analysis flexibility and inferential stability

Episode 01 now asks which exact-kernel, stage, masking, scaling, estimation,
aggregation, residual, or reliability mechanism explains S0-to-S8 map change
and transports from NARPS to one external mixed-gambles dataset. The two old
NARPS runs remain frozen, fully exposed priors; they count as no new evidence
and do not license an unrestricted multiverse. One configuration must be locked
before a permission-separated `ds000005` audit.

### Causal learning mechanisms under closed-loop perturbation

Formal Episode 02 asks whether physiological mesolimbic dopamine gates the
magnitude of an otherwise signed policy update rather than serving as the
signed teaching or reinforcement signal itself. Its bounded contract uses nine
development controls, an intended one-shot primary comparison of six
`stimLick-` and five `stimLick+` mice, and a non-rescuing four-mouse
supraphysiological boundary diagnostic. Exact role-pack materialization passed
integrity checks, but the handoff remains `materialized_unsealed`: same-UID
source readability and network reacquisition defeat the confidentiality
firewall. Outcome-blind generic calibration v2 returned `selected_rule: null`
(0 of 216 eligible), so the current contract authorizes neither an audit opening nor
a positive mechanistic terminal. Endpoint/time semantics, a documented
assignment mechanism or presigned conditional-exchangeability basis,
endpoint-faithful calibration with an absolute raw-unit margin, scientist
signoff, an author-code port/configuration and resource profile, and a
permission-separated evaluator remain blockers for opening the audit. This can
be only a publication-exposed reanalysis, not independent replication.

### Visual-model relations under measurement contracts

Episode 17 asks which controlled pairwise visual-model relations remain stable
under isolated beta, voxel-support, and ceiling-treatment changes, which are
ROI- or semantic-stratum-conditional, and which are below the resolution of
current fMRI data. CNeuroMod-only concept roles reserve 480 concepts for
development, 120 for support/calibration, and 120 for a direct,
no-refit audit in the same four participants. This is held-out-stimulus
replication, not cross-dataset, new-participant, or population evidence.

### Semantics and cross-dataset brain-map geometry

The new Episode 03 asks whether methods-derived condition semantics predict
map geometry in held-out datasets beyond broad task-family prototypes. Its
corpus was exposed during prior development, so this is discovery; future
confirmation needs prospectively sealed datasets.

### Cerebellar topography versus response geometry

Episode 15 asks which constrained explanation adequately predicts reliable
between-person variation in 18 Task-B-only cerebellar maps from anatomy and
Task A: hierarchical parcel membership, one smooth relocation field, a
certified isometric correspondence, or bounded stable non-isometry. Its direct
falsifier is prediction of participant-specific condition-geometry deviations,
not another demonstration that Task-A individualization can transfer. MDTB is
public and Task A/B differ by session, so the 12-participant one-shot holdout
is internal mechanistic evidence, not external replication or proof of task
dependence.

### Measurement, regional organization, acquisition policy, and transfer

The LFP line now separates three primary questions: reproducible regional
frequency-latent profiles, post-pilot electrode retention/trial allocation,
and cross-session mapping transfer. They share source assets and exposure
history but not terminal estimands. Measurement validity should precede
portability claims.

### Cell-resolved organization beyond coarse brain regions

The morphology line tests three distinct objects: local-to-distal prediction,
residual co-projection combinations, and reusable residual morphology modes
versus continuous-unimodal anatomical gradients. Shared cells require one
exposure and whole-brain split ledger; three analyses of one release are not
three independent replications. Without an independent molecular label, the
last question cannot identify neuron types.

### Meta-science of autonomous analysis

Across many episodes, the campaign asks whether bounded agents generate
sharper, reproducible tests while preserving failures and exposure firewalls,
or merely automate analysis selection. A technically complete run is not
scientific acceptance.
