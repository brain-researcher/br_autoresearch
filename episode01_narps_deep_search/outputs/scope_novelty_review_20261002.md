# EP01 scope, novelty, and clarity — 2026-10-02

Design review only; no data or scientific outcome was opened.

## Question and contribution tier

Do specified decision-state and response controls move conditional gain/loss
maps reproducibly, and do those directions describe the public NARPS analyst
maps? Retain the computational/controlled-model reanalysis plus descriptive
analysis-ecology scope. It is a candidate substantive methods contribution,
not a new discovery of loss aversion or independent three-study confirmation.

## Closest checked primary work

- [Botvinik-Nezer et al., 2020](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-020-2314-9)
  established many-team map and decision variation. EP01 must identify a
  particular cognitive-specification structure, not relabel generic variability.
- [Zhao et al., 2020](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cogpsych.2020.101331)
  already decomposed utility weighting, fixed utility bias, and pre-valuation
  bias with choice/RT models.
- [Bobadilla-Suarez et al., 2020](https://www.nature.com/articles/s42003-020-01315-3)
  already related subjective value and decision entropy in NARPS fMRI.

The proposed additional result is the controlled specification-to-map link and
its descriptive relation to declared analyst choices. These sources support
that positioning, not an exhaustive claim of priority.

## Repairs and remaining uncertainty

The opening no longer attributes a shared-estimand assumption to prior work
without evidence. The paper plan no longer calls the combined same-participant
layers a cross-validated contribution by themselves. Conditional slopes remain
conditional slopes; displacement maps are not isolated cognitive processes.

Behavioral recoverability, design efficiency, stable map bases, and incremental
method-coordinate prediction still need empirical evaluation. No prose can
guarantee that this connection exists or yields a paper. The current model
families, thresholds, data roles, execution program, and stop rules are unchanged.
