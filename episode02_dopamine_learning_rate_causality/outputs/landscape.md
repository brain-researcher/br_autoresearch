# Landscape projection

EP02 is a proposed publication-exposed mechanistic-discrimination reanalysis,
not an observed result. The source paper's adaptive-rate account is prior
knowledge. The proposed contribution is to establish how much a locked
alternative-prediction panel and whole-mouse protocol contrast do or do not
resolve. A positive target-consistency terminal is not unique learning-rule
identification; a repeated published direction alone does not supply novelty.

No adaptive-search realization, audit result, mechanism claim, or canonical
Landscape transition has occurred.

The current release also does not identify the documented small-cohort
randomization blocks or post-collection exclusions, so an unblocked 462-label
calculation cannot be promoted to a randomized-design or ITT result.

The 2026-09-30 paper plan and conceptual figure are completed writing artifacts,
not new empirical evidence, acceptance, or a campaign-level state transition.
