# EP02 scope, novelty, and clarity — 2026-10-02

Design/literature review only. Keep the current question and operative policy.

## Question and honest contribution tier

What does the released contrast between two closed-loop stimulation protocols
identify about control-qualified learning rules? A finite-panel discrimination
or identification-limit reanalysis is defensible. Rediscovering the published
direction or fitting control behavior better is not a new dopamine mechanism.

  already proposes the adaptive-rate interpretation and reports the calibrated

- [FLEX, 2024](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-024-50205-3) supplies a
  temporal-representation account outside the active finite panel.
- [Dopaminergic action prediction errors, 2025](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-025-09008-9)
  supplies a value-free teaching account in tail-striatal stimulus–action
  learning. Its circuit/task differs from this mesolimbic trace-conditioning
  study; it is context, not a direct result that EP02 adjudicates.

The read-only BR publication search for dopamine/learning-rate work returned
broad neighboring studies rather than direct new evidence on this release.
It did not change the design or establish a novelty verdict.

## Action and remaining uncertainty

Added the 2025 neighboring account to the paper's generalization boundary.
Retained the raw acquisition-gain estimand, not a latent-rate measurement;
retained the distinction between protocol contrast and unique mechanism.
No new rival, threshold, experiment, or gate is introduced.

Endpoint semantics, assignment/attrition support, calibrated raw margins, and
actual separation among locked predictions remain unresolved. With eleven
primary mice, imprecision is plausible; nonrejection is not equivalence. A
standalone paper remains conditional on a substantive discrimination or
identification-limit result, not on improved prose or documentation.
