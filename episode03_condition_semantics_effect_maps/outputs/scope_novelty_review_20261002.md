# EP03 scope, novelty, and clarity — 2026-10-02

Prospective design amendment; no current-contract map computation has run.

## Question and contribution tier

Does a methods-only experimental comparison add transferable normalized-map
information beyond an oriented task-family prototype? Retain this evaluation
question and broad registered representation/readout search. It is a candidate
incremental-information study, not a new text-to-brain architecture.

## Checked primary overlap

[Text2Brain, 2022](https://arxiv.org/abs/2208.00840),
[NeuroConText, 2024](https://papers.miccai.org/miccai-2024/560-Paper3550.html),
and [NeuroConText, 2026](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41852941/)
already establish text-to-map generation or contrastive reconstruction.
The existing plan also addresses NeuroQuery and NeuroVLM. The proposed
additional evidence is incremental information beyond a meaningful family
comparator under independent-group, neighbor-exclusion, and full-procedure
null evaluation; these references do not establish global priority for it.

## Actual repair

An unchanged family prototype `p` plus an antisymmetric residual gives `p+r`
and `p-r`, not opposite predictions. The Goal, data contract, and policy now
specify a map-blind canonical ordering and polarity `s`. Training uses `s Z`,
and scoring restores `s` to both the baseline and the **whole** model prediction:
`s p` versus `s[p+r]`. Reversed copies remain one comparison. Ordering uses
experimental roles/ontology, never a favorable target-map sign. No prototype
is silently canceled or replaced with zero.

Reversal is therefore an implementation invariant, not evidence of semantic
understanding. Oriented prototype strength and residual reliability must be
visible so arbitrary cancellation cannot manufacture the apparent increment.
The existing score, operator search, nulls, budgets, and access roles remain.

## Empirical limits

The exposed 51-group corpus remains development-only; the handoff and a fresh
audit corpus are absent. Family support, residual reliability, uncertainty,
and meaningful gain still require the existing pre-search decisions. A null
cannot prove family sufficiency without useful precision. The existing missing
ASTRA projection is not a new execution gate and is not repaired by this
scientific-writing amendment.
