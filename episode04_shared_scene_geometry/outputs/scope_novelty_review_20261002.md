# EP04 scope, novelty, and clarity — 2026-10-02

Scientific-positioning revision only. Direct native embedding–fMRI RSA remains
the user's requested target; no reproduction or learned primary mapping added.

## Question and contribution tier

Does the selected VLM's native-geometry advantage over the fixed DINOv2 survive
the image-distribution boundary on **within-category** OOD comparisons?
This can yield a useful boundary/generalization benchmark. It is not currently
an assured mechanism paper or a discovery that VLMs align with visual cortex.

## Closest checked primary work

- [Combined model and human-lesion study, Nature Human Behaviour](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41562-025-02357-5)
  reports CLIP correspondence across four fMRI datasets, comparisons with visual
  models, and lesion evidence. Generic VLM advantage and a language-related
  interpretation already have substantial precedent.
- [BrainSAIL, ICLR 2025](https://proceedings.iclr.cc/paper_files/paper/2025/file/73af055566f5514b9863315133b84eda-Paper-Conference.pdf)
  compares CLIP/DINO/SigLIP-based encoding representations. EP04's primary is
  instead direct native geometry, but this difference alone does not make a
  three-model ranking a substantive contribution.

## Repair and exact claim

The Goal now foregrounds the existing within-category OOD criterion and names
the closest overlap. Between-category separation cannot drive that primary
endpoint; low-level partial RSA remains required. The registered 0.02 Fisher-z
advantage, participant and influence rules, full model panel, and single audit
are unchanged. OOD and development are different estimands: passing both does
not estimate a shift interaction or percent retained alignment.

Unmatched training datasets and architectures prevent a causal language-
supervision claim. Five repeated participants test stimulus shift, not a
participant-population effect. Feature coverage, eligible ROI support, actual
alignment, and the OOD exposure condition still require the existing evidence.
No new data, model, experiment, access right, or manuscript-quality guarantee
follows from this review.
