## Scientifically defensible next choices, without adoption

The [linear allocation assessment](primary_post_blend_next_step_assessment.md)
asks a legitimate question: whether W/HW mixing benefits from a stable early/late
allocation. H2W's loss does not establish such a time course. Its five ramps,
other-fifteen-session selection, literal zero-slope reuse and uncharged reversal
contrast are distinct from the old temporal tensor. The mean 0.5 constraint is
an exposed-development adaptation; it does not match prediction amplitude or
capacity. A formal proposal must freeze ties, old/H2 anchors, duplicate rules
and resources. One distinct policy adds at most one candidate, no automatic
alternative/null charge.

Two other allowed directions have different scientific purposes:

- **Support robustness of the actual blend.** I/R's loss to full HW but
  advantage over random sets supplies a concrete question that does not transfer
  automatically to the mixture. Rebuild both component states on selected
  predeclared channel/trial conditions, preserving the policy's nested selector;
  do not combine full-W with reduced-HW predictions. Use common-target direct
  comparisons and disclose baseline changes when trials change. This is an
  optional, nonpromotable support stress for the ineligible blend, not a reason
  to repeat every old condition automatically.
- **Covariance-aware supervised compression.** A genuinely deflated PLS
  regression family could ask whether jointly predictive directions lost by
  marginal top-16 or rank-two raw covariance-SVD selection are recoverable
  without full spatial complexity. GOAL explicitly permits PLS. This would
  need an exact algorithm, small finite component schedule, native residual
  loss, training-only component construction, own matched target-label
  controls and independent/matched-penalty anchors. It must demonstrate its
  distinction from the already completed covariance-SVD/ridge recipe before
  adoption; calling that old basis “PLS” again is zero new science. This is a
  lower-confidence option, not evidence of a missing minimum family or a
  promised improvement over the full weighted models.

Expanding lag/window/degree grids, cycling through bands, changing prior floors
or weakening stopped elastic-net convergence criteria currently has less
justification. Each next proposal needs an unresolved scientific distinction,
a relevant control and a falsifiable interpretation. This audit selects none;
the completed I/R evidence does not authorize promotion.
