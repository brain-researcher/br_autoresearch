## Question and contribution tier

Which task-defined population component benefits from a source-animal frequency
rule in the target animal beyond pooled/swapped rules? Retain EP06 as an EP07
component, not another independent LFP-to-spikes paper.

already establishes region/frequency-dependent LFP–population relationships in
this release. [Safaie et al., 2023](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-023-06714-0)
establishes motor-population transfer across animals. The candidate addition
is the useful consequence of a source frequency rule for a specified projected
component, not generic regional differences or cross-animal latent alignment.

- Any numerically unscoreable rival: the full comparison is unresolved; no
  nomination is manufactured by removing it.
- All rivals measured: sign consistency applies to each potential nominee.
  Select the largest median among eligible candidates; mixed-sign rivals stay
  reported without vetoing another candidate. If none qualifies, no nominee.

of four. This is the best eligible joint-region component, not a global
