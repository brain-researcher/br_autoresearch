## Why this comparison and what authorizes the new roles

Root retains residual initial marginal selection as the incumbent. The raw
initializer challenger has only+0.000664Q overall, below the0.001 progress
resolution, and loses in Mihili. Earlier alternative selectors and controls
all inherited the same32 initializer trials. The completed
[arrival-order comparison](trial_arrival_order_proposal.md) explicitly kept
“original32initializer identities and order” and reordered only positions4:12.
It therefore did not test initializer robustness.

The scientist-approved [open design](open_exploration_design.md) permits
“initialization strategies on development sessions,” and states that fitting
and timing declarations are “per-comparison validity commitments, not an early
freeze of the entire search.” Its explicit limit is: “A changed initializer is
a different experiment; its endpoints and terminal decision need their own
prospective specification.” The operative policy leaves
`model_summary_initializer_menu_closed: false` while preserving data roles,
actual information timing, shared budgets and one held opening. This draft
prospectively specifies that different experiment. Adoption would authorize
new fitting subroles inside the **existing exact96-trial fitting whitelist**;
it would not authorize choosing a new whitelist or evaluation set, rewriting
frozen old roles, or silently applying new roles to old comparisons.

The original optional16-trial family coverage is not a universal requirement
of this branch. This comparison keeps the currently qualified32-trial paid
initializer and prediction metric. It asks whether its target-correspondence
advantage survives prespecified changes in initializer identity and the
resulting balanced acquisition path. It does not isolate initializer identity
from every later-prefix/CV change or test next-direction benefit.

## Honest averaging with different prefixes and nulls

Each order/cell uses its own true acquired-training global Y mean/raw-Y SD
and training-global-null denominator, plus its own training-only direction D.
Within that order/cell all five controls share the target denominator: average
their correct/wrong/native SSEs first and derive the single control Q and
pairing readouts. Then give each of the **three order-specific Q values equal
weight** at the matched resource cell. Do not pool SSE across orders, divide
by an averaged denominator, average predictions, or treat averaged cells as
if one shared scalar denominator generated them. Retain full per-order cells,
SSEs, nulls, seed metadata and fitted models; averaged cells explicitly declare
mean-of-three-Q aggregation and omit nonexistent common-SSE/capacity metadata.

Average the20 cells equally within each session, four sessions equally within
animal, then the two animals equally. For each seed, average its within-order
Q equally across all three orders before animal summaries. Report all order,
seed, cell, session and animal contrasts, plus fixed-prediction session
omissions without fitting. Orders/seeds are computational conditions with
overlapping fitting rows and common evaluation outcomes, not extra animals,
independent confirmations or a confidence distribution.

Primary evidence is candidate minus mean-five-control Q, averaged over the
three orders, with the familiar directional screen requiring strictly positive
means in both animals. A separate stronger robustness screen requires that
contrast be positive in **both animal means in each of all three orders**;
ties fail either screen. A positive average with any failing order supports
only an average correspondence result, not stable initializer robustness.
Report candidate minus D descriptively and all omission/seed sign changes.
No new meaningful-effect threshold or automatic policy promotion is proposed;
the existing0.001-Q search-resolution and shared stopping policy remain.
The six order-by-animal sign requirements are an initializer-sensitivity
interpretation only: passing them does not automatically create a new feasible
frontier point, reset patience, or promote the policy. No new frontier-reset
rule is adopted here; the existing0.001 same-support incumbent-improvement
rule remains, and the first Q under a new order is neither an incumbent-
comparable improvement nor a patience reset.
