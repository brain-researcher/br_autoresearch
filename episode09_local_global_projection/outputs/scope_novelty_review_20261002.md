# EP09 scope, novelty and clarity review — 2026-10-02

Outcome-blind design/literature review; no payload, candidate or held-out result
was inspected for this review.

## Question and defensible contribution

Within independently defined cortical IT strata, does a neuron's correct
dendritic residual predict its exact distal targets better than a coherent
residual from a tightly matched donor? The prospective contribution is a
reproducible **dendritic-scale × exact-target pattern of that own-versus-donor
advantage**, not merely improved prediction from morphology or extra controls.

## Closest checked primary literature

- [Peng et al. 2021](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-021-03941-1)
  documents whole-neuron projection diversity and supplies much of the source
  lineage. Reanalysis of that lineage is not independent replication.
- [Gao et al. 2023](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41593-023-01339-y)
  already studies dendrite–axon correspondence and its exceptions.
- [Liu et al. 2024](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-024-54745-6)
  supplies refined whole-brain morphology and the SEU-A1876 context.
- [Sorensen et al. 2026](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-026-10424-8)
  already links multimodal neuronal identity, morphology and projection
  targets. Predicting target identity from morphology is not an unoccupied
  question.

The current own-versus-coherent-donor estimand is meaningfully narrower than
these broad correspondences. It remains a candidate contribution, not proof
that no prior study contains a related contrast.

## Interpretation repair

Equivalence of correct and donor dendrites establishes no meaningful
own-dendrite advantage within the tested margin and representation. It does
not identify shared class or neighborhood organization as the cause. GOAL and
the paper decision table now state that limited conclusion.

Conversely, a correct-dendrite advantage is residual association under the
available strata; unmeasured finer subtypes can still explain it. The study
does not identify cellular individuality, developmental mechanisms or synapses.

## What remains empirical

Existing contracts still require outcome-independent IT membership, defensible
biological groups, exact-target observability and donor support. The central
burden is whether native residual information and its scale × target structure
replicate across those groups, at the declared margins. Source overlap limits
independence; stricter matching cannot create new animals or cell classes.

No endpoint, margin, access boundary, scoring rule or ASTRA dependency is
changed here. These remaining requirements are inherited scientific support,
not new review gates.
