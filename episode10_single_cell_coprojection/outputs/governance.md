# Governance

The v2 design is not ready for launch. Its first stage is a feasibility audit
of source support, sample lineage, target observation, cross-resource
comparability, and computation. Dataset roles and the conditional
branch-coverage rule must be resolved before outcomes are analyzed. These
files do not report or approve a scientific result. The shared-target anatomy
endpoint is secondary and can activate only after A, B, C, its metric, and its
held-out prediction are frozen; it does not alter the primary split, budget,
stopping thresholds, null calibration, or promotion rule.
