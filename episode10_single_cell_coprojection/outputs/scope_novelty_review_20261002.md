# EP10 scope, novelty and clarity review — 2026-10-02

Writing/literature review only. The frozen v3 development execution and the
planned biological follow-up are different studies; nothing here activates
the latter or modifies the former.

## Question and closest evidence

Does positively observed co-target context distinguish terminal organization
within a shared target, beyond soma, layer and flexible source geometry?

- [Yuan et al. 2024](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-024-52756-x)
  already associates additional projection destinations with target-layer
  organization. Its auditory-source comparison includes lateral-target
  neurons with versus without medial co-projection. This is a named known
  precedent, not an EP10 discovery or an eligible positive-only contrast.
- [Gao et al. 2026, online 2025](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41253150/)
  already describes projection-defined subtypes and diverse terminal arbors
  in the resource used by active EP10. Reusing its trees is not replication.
- [Falasconi et al. 2026](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42777707/)
  identifies motor modules, two spatial axes and subcortical convergence/
  divergence involving striatum, thalamus and brainstem. Source organization
  is a strong competing explanation, not a new phenomenon EP10 can claim.

The generic proposition that co-projection and termination covary is occupied.
Neither switching atlas labels nor adding a source-geometry control alone
creates a paper-level contribution.

## Concrete biological interpretation, without a phantom pathway

A meaningful prospective distinction is: when source-defined motor pathways
converge on a common downstream region, are their terminal implementations
explained by source position/module alone, or does positive co-target context
identify an additional organization that a pooled target description misses?
This is an inference from the cited anatomy, not a new published gap or result.
Full-tree entry routes and branch organization can explain a particular
difference; they are not independent validation.

The literature names biologically relevant downstream systems but does not
establish an eligible new A/B/C comparison in EP10's frozen MOp cohort.
Common source-space support, sufficiently distinct positive contexts and their
overlap depend on permitted development observations. Published source-paper
analyses must then be compared against that actual candidate. Choosing a
striatal parcel or two cortical names now would add specificity without
evidence. The next scientific choice legitimately needs development support,
not more caveats or an arbitrary shortlist.

The Yuan example cannot simply be imported: a context defined by an unobserved
other target is not allowed here. B-positive and C-positive contexts may
overlap; A+B+C neurons remain dependent observations in both summaries.

## Assessment and remaining burden

Keep the current design. Its potential contribution is a consequential
pathway-specific prediction: a meaningful within-target contrast that
reproduces in new biological groups, survives the measured source alternatives,
and changes an explicit interpretation of target pooling. No such candidate
or novel biological result is established by this review. A supported case may
still be replication/extension rather than a new paper.

The active v3 conditional recorded-positive-pattern score, controller nulls,
numerical margins, budgets and final-access limits remain exactly as recorded.
They do not calibrate the new terminal endpoint. The prospective follow-up's
measurement, contrast, inference and access decisions remain its own work.
No final/unassigned outcomes, terminal profiles or candidate data were read,
and no job or runtime state was queried for this review.
