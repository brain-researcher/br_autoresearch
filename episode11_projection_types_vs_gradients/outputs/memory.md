## Frozen interpretation decisions

- The present 12-development/8-audit requirement is design-specific and must
  be justified by precision and synthetic recovery; it is not a universal
  scientific threshold.
- Stage 0 freezes the estimand, roles, candidate grammar, hyperparameter ranges,
  scoring, and selection rules. Registered noise, nuisance, regularization,
  smoothness, latent-dimension, and candidate choices may be learned from
  animal-held-out development outcomes.
- Filesystem readability is not exposure. Logged trusted hashing, format
  validation, identity joining, and allow-list redaction without visible values
  are not candidate outcome exposure. Candidate-visible projection information
  is exposure; missing access records make affected animals ineligible as fresh
  audit.
- `closed_insufficient_source_support` would describe a current data--design
  mismatch, not absence of projection groups or permanent abandonment of EP11.
