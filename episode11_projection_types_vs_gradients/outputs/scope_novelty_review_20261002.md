# EP11 scope, novelty and clarity review — 2026-10-02

Design/literature review only. Runtime history below the new experiment-log
entry remains the existing record, not a newly verified scheduler report.

## Question and prior-work boundary

At comparable locations in SSp-tr, does a reusable regional axon-allocation
description add useful held-out prediction beyond a flexible continuous
description? This is model-relative usefulness, not proof of discrete neuronal
ontology.

- [Peng et al. 2021](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-021-03941-1)
  already establishes projection diversity and spatial organization.
- [Timonidis et al. 2023](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/neuroinformatics/articles/10.3389/fninf.2023.1272243/full)
  studies motifs along a morphological continuum in somatosensory thalamus.
  It is a nearby precedent, not the same SSp-tr question; combining motifs
  and gradients is nevertheless not a new general idea.
- [Gao et al. 2026, online 2025](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41253150/)
  already classifies projection-defined subtypes in EP11's source resource.
  Re-clustering that resource or using its axon-derived classes as independent
  validation would not establish a new result.

## Assessment: keep

The proposed contribution is a **named, anatomically interpretable allocation
rule that transfers on common source-space support beyond the qualified
continuum**. Strong continuous-model evidence can instead bound the usefulness
of the tested group description. These are worthwhile potential findings,
not guaranteed papers or evidence that all continuous/discrete alternatives
have been exhausted.

The current plan already makes the essential distinctions: the 56-coordinate
outcome is relative regional axon centerline length, including passing
grey-matter axon, not synapse counts or terminal mass; latent groups are
integrated out for new-neuron scoring, not treated as independently known
labels. No additional narrowing or new anatomical target is adopted here.

## Remaining empirical burden

The 206-cell, 36-sample inventory does not certify 36 independent animals.
The existing 12/8/16 role design and actual group-level uncertainty determine
what can transfer. More simulated draws or reconstructed cells do not supply
more independent animals. A named rule must be chosen in permitted development
data and evaluated under the unchanged access and inference rules.

Existing documentation reports unresolved evaluator recovery/precision work;
this review neither adjudicates its latest attempt nor declares feasibility.
Frozen B/P/C/D/H families, reference selection, nulls, margins and runtime
contracts stay untouched. A different, narrower estimand would require its own
scientific decision, not a silent writing repair.
