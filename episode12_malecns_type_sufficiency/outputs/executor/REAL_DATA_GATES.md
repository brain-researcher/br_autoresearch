# EP12 real-data enablement gates

Status: unresolved by design. These gates are not defaults and no values are
chosen here.

The synthetic controller can be qualified without deciding the items below.
Opening outcome-revealing development data cannot begin until an executable
contract freezes them and a scientist resolves any consequential choice that
cannot be derived from the existing Goal and search policy.

## Scientific and statistical choices still required

- the exact annotation-only eligibility rule and deterministic whole-type
  split seed;
- concrete T, U, and M estimators, their capacity-matching rule, and the
  numerical unimodality test for U;
- the real balanced covering array and readiness-rejection rules;
- the development selection, tie-breaking, and “comparably supported” rules;
- the simultaneous whole-type uncertainty implementation and its numerical
  replay tolerance;
- executable criteria for mode separation, prevalence, cross-side matching,
  partner identifiability, and every required control;
- the adequacy-sensitivity generator grid spanning eligible sizes and
  missingness, without adding an outcome-driven support threshold;
- the fitted-T/U null-generator comparison rule, frozen seeds, proposal model,
  prompt, sampling settings, and deterministic replay checks;
- the development-only main-figure example rule; and
- storage/failure ceilings, the exact retryable pre-output failures, and the
  final evaluator output allowlist.
- the frozen support rule and reason-code schema that retain an assigned final
  type as explicitly unscorable in both reciprocal directions. The current
  synthetic final fixture scores every type and does not qualify that branch.

## Engineering evidence still required

- implementation and synthetic qualification of the actual T/U/M estimators;
- endpoint-mass accounting for unknown, untyped, fragment, proofreading,
  missing-annotation, and out-of-vocabulary bins;
- source and schema authentication without reading connectivity outcomes;
- a cost profile showing the observed search plus 99 complete null searches
  fits the fixed 1,000 CPU-core-hour ceiling before outcome access;
- Slurm packaging for expensive work; and
- a locked exposure, role, seed, code, environment, and evaluator manifest.

Until these gates close, a synthetic controller terminal demonstrates only
state-machine behavior. It cannot support an EP12 scientific conclusion,
canonical acceptance, reward, or Landscape transition.
