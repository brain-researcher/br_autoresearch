# Observed outgoing composition in one male fly

All 816 already-open development types are shown, without selecting types by their outcomes. A: each point compares left and right mean total-variation (TV) distances from positive-mass neuron profiles to their own neuron-equal type-side centroid; the dashed line marks equality. B: the complete distribution of TV between those two centroids, in fixed 0.05-wide bins. C: all 1,632 type-side observations show positive-mass support and the neuron-equal fraction of outgoing composition assigned to untyped/status or missing-annotation partner bins.

The accounting covers 84,735 neurons: 84,609 have positive outgoing mass, while 126 have undefined composition and are explicitly counted rather than imputed. Every type has positive-mass profiles on both sides. All 11,771 raw partner bins, including unknown mass, remain in the composition denominator.

These are in-sample summaries within one specimen. Each neuron contributes to its own centroid, so dispersion depends on sample size.
