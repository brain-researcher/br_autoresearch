# Memory

Independent design review found that the original 48--120-trial adaptive
search mixed atlas, representation, reducer, predictor, nonlinearity, and
ensemble choices, so a winner could not support an interpretable
representation claim.

The earlier operative contract became a fixed four-arm comparison with one predictor, one probe, one
primary domain-macro age estimand, and a conditional public holdout. The three
RAMP splits remain overlapping robustness views, not replications. Linear site
decodability is a guardrail, not proof of scanner invariance.

September 30: four-arm ridge comparison alone is now explicitly a limited
methods comparison, not a new age/site-removal discovery. Dinsdale 2021 already
describes the scanner/age support tradeoff. The planned scientific diagnostic
asks whether discarded directions predict age within domains and at unseen
domains on common support, beside matched controls and a site-beyond-age probe.
The two age views are necessary because domain-average age prediction can
imitate transferable utility. This amendment is not active or fully frozen.

## Later September 30 representation refocusing

The scientist restored the original open representation question. Keep the
four-arm contract as the existing reference, not as the limit of scientific
design. Site removal and its diagnostic are optional branches.

A full bilateral mean/difference representation is invertible. Under matched
scaling/metric, orthonormal rotation with isotropic ridge is equivalent; gains
after re-standardization, block penalties or truncation concern inductive bias
or compression, not additional information. Test anatomical grouping against
generic/shuffled alternatives and keep predictor changes distinguishable.

Shared/specific, bilateral and multiscale ideas have direct precedents. A new
paper requires a discriminating transfer result, not merely an expanded search
or a renamed representation. ROI pooling is not physical surface coarse-graining.
