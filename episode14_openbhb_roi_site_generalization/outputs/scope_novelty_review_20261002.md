# EP14 scope, novelty and clarity review — 2026-10-02

Design/literature review; no participant data, public-validation labels or
model outcomes were inspected.

## Question

Which anatomical representations and shallow predictors make chronological-
age prediction transfer across acquisition domains, and what explains an
advantage beyond generic compression, regularization or capacity?

The scientific object is the representation. Site/acquisition supplies the
generalization environment; removing site information is not the main goal.
Keep the recently restored broad search.

## Closest checked primary literature

- [Zhao et al. 2019](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6819257/)
  already separates shared and morphometric-family-specific variation and
  evaluates age prediction across cohorts.
- [Korbmacher et al. 2024](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-024-45282-3)
  already studies hemispheric brain-age representations and asymmetry.
- [Leiberg et al. 2025](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12205997/)
  studies complementary information at physical cortical scales. Pooling
  existing ROI summaries does not implement the same surface operation.

These precedents rule out generic novelty claims for decomposition, bilateral
features or scale-dependent prediction. Existing OpenBHB benchmarking also
makes acquisition-shift age prediction an established task; a small MAE win
alone is a predictive-methods result, not a new anatomical principle.

## Assessment: keep broad exploration

The defensible potential contribution is a discriminating transfer pattern:
which anatomical organization helps, under what observed conditions, and
whether a matched generic or shuffled alternative explains the same benefit.
For bilateral structure, full invertible change of basis adds no information;
orthonormal rotation with the same isotropic ridge metric is an equivalence
reference. Different block penalties test an anatomical regularization prior.
For grouped compression, matched dimension, fitting opportunity and randomized
grouping help distinguish organization from generic shrinkage.

These are starting arguments, not a mandatory two-branch sequence or fixed
candidate menu. Measurement families, granularity, reducers and shallow
predictors remain open. No branch is declared novel or biologically preferred
before data. A result explained by generic regularization is still reportable
without claiming anatomical specificity.

## Remaining empirical burden and scope

The proposed broad search is not the operative frozen R0–R3 comparison.
Its future executable amendment must preserve prospective selection and
existing access limits; this review does not instantiate it. The original
reference's endpoints, site guardrail, margins and ASTRA program are unchanged.

Existing documents record 3,227 exposed development participants and 757
conditional public-validation rows. Overlapping views do not create new
participants, and participant resampling does not create acquisition domains.
Transfer claims need the actual domain-wise evidence and legitimate holdout
status, not a new representation name.

No finding establishes a biological aging mechanism. No further prose
reframing is required now: the next scientific design task is broad candidate
coverage with interpretable comparisons, followed by authorized empirical
work—not another turn toward site removal.
