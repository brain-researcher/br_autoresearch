# EP15 scope, novelty and clarity review — 2026-10-02

Question: can anatomy and Task A predict a person's unseen B-only condition geometry, and distinguish parcel changes, smooth relocation, geometry-preserving remapping and bounded geometry change?

## Closest work and the remaining contribution

[King et al. (2019)](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41593-019-0436-x) establishes the MDTB functional-organization background. [Nettekoven et al. (2024)](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-024-52371-w) and the [2026 Nettekoven preprint](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.03.09.710558v1) make individual cerebellar organization and prediction a particularly close neighborhood. Neither personalization nor computing task geometry is an available novelty claim.

The defensible contribution is prospective prediction of the signed full geometry deviation for 18 B-only conditions, with competing constrained spatial accounts required to explain the same maps and geometry. Map accuracy alone does not establish individual geometry change. M1 and M2 can change voxel-weighted geometry too; M4 must distinguish itself from them, not just from paired M3.

## Decision and repair

Keep the scientific comparison and its open within-class exploration. The paper-plan branch formerly allowed an equally predictive spatial account to win because it was simpler. That conflicted with the non-nested, signature-based decision: only a uniquely supported account earns that interpretation; several adequate accounts mean non-identifiability. The repaired wording also calls these statistical accounts, not identified biological mechanisms.

A compelling paper remains conditional on reliable individual variation, discriminating signatures and adequate audit precision. Task set and session are not independently manipulated, and these are publication-exposed MDTB participants, not a new cohort. Failure to distinguish models is an informative boundary, not evidence of biological equivalence or a guaranteed standalone paper.

No model family, data role, margin, search budget or execution permission changes. This review used design documents and source literature only; no neural payload or closed outcome was opened.
