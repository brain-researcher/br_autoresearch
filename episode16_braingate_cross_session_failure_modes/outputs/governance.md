# Study boundaries

EP16 is currently authorized only as a documented study design. The current
files do not authorize opening held-out neural outcomes or running the full
search.

## Decisions already made

- The participant is the biological unit.
- Six people are used for development and three for final held-out evaluation.
- Near gaps are 1–30 days and long gaps are at least 180 days.
- The primary low-budget recalibration uses 32 labelled trials.
- The same conclusion must hold separately in all three held-out people.

## Decisions still required before neural scoring

- final participant roles after the outcome-blind support and exposure checks;
- the onset offset and target-schedule tolerance;
- channel-quality, numerical stability, and method-complexity limits;
- all still-empty scientific margins in `../SEARCH_POLICY.yaml`; and
- successful synthetic checks of the analysis and leakage controls.

No scientific acceptance, external review decision, or permission to use a
held-out result is implied by this summary.
