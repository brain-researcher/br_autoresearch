# Research landscape note

## What earlier work already establishes

BrainGate studies have shown long-term changes in recording quality and decoder
performance, benefits from recalibration, and cases where latent alignment
improves stability. EP16 cannot claim that drift exists or that calibration can
help as new findings.

## The open gap

What remains unresolved is whether one common, budget-matched analysis can
separate three operational explanations across several participants:

- less direction signal recoverable within later sessions;
- sufficiency of observable changes in the recording; and
- a mapping mismatch repairable with 32 labelled trials.

## Current contribution status

This is a proposed contribution, not a finding. No experiment has started and
no result has been added to the research landscape.
