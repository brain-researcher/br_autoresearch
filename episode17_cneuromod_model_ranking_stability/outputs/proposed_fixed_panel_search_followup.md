## The decision

Replace the unreachable adaptive-search completion requirements with one
complete, fixed **20-trial panel**: the three registered promotion pairs
P1/P2/P3 on all five registered directed measurement edges (15 trials), plus
the registered trained-versus-random falsifier F1 on those same five edges
(5 trials). Require every trial to satisfy the unchanged validity rules.

This changes the scientific search design. It does not establish that the
original adaptive search was completed, that 20 trials are equivalent to 32,
or that an observed result is positive. Scientist adoption and corresponding
operative-record updates are required before this proposal can govern search
completion or a subsequent audit decision.

## Why the current completion rule is unreachable

The [search policy](../SEARCH_POLICY.yaml) defines one trial as one model pair
by one directed measurement edge across all four participants. It requires
15 initial trials, 32–72 valid trials overall, two adaptive successor cycles,
two incumbent/challenger decisions and the specified patience rule. A
successor must change exactly one scientific operator and name its scored
parent, directional prediction, competing explanation, native falsifier,
information gain/cost and retirement condition.

The adopted [model registry](model_pair_registry_specification.md) names only
P1, P2, P3 and F1 and explicitly proposes no optional fourth promotion pair.
The adopted [measurement specification](measurement_contract_specification.md)
and frozen family001 contain seven primary endpoints and exactly five
directed edges. These yield at most 20 distinct registered pair-by-edge
trials. The executed initial computations do not themselves define further
adaptive successors.

The permitted sensitivities do not supply an additional registered trial
menu or counting rule. Correlation/CKA score scales, participant/ROI splits,
concept blocks, folds, images, voxels and anatomical-bank members cannot
inflate trial count. Reversing signs or edge direction, repeating the same
calculation or calling two interval families two trials adds no new credit.
Changing frozen supports, thresholds, model pairs or eligible endpoint
families to manufacture successors would require its own explicit decision.

Thus the unresolved issue is a mismatch between the frozen finite menu and
the search-completion rule. It is not missing compute capacity or permission
to inspect private results. All 20 completed trials would still be 12 short
of the original minimum. Five F1 trials also cannot supply seven falsifiers
among 17 postcoverage trials if the original 32-trial minimum were retained.

## Exact proposed replacement

1. Preserve the original trial atom and all 15 P1/P2/P3 initial pair-by-edge
   trials. Require F1 on the same five edges as the five additional mandatory
   native falsifiers. F1 remains nonpromotion-eligible.
2. Replace the 32–72 valid-trial requirement with completion of these exact
   20 distinct valid trials. No substituted pair, extra checkpoint/seed,
   additional ROI, alternative support or replacement trial is authorized.
3. Replace the minimum two adaptive successor cycles, minimum two
   incumbent/challenger decisions and adaptive patience requirement with
   completion of the whole fixed panel. There is no adaptive branch-retirement
   process under this route and no claim that its former retirement criteria
   were satisfied.
4. Preserve the falsification intent by requiring all five F1 edges. After
   the 15 initial promotion-pair trials, every additional trial is a native
   falsifier: 5/5, exceeding the original 40% postcoverage fraction. This is
   fixed-panel falsification, not evidence of adaptive falsifier selection.
5. Review all 20 trials before selecting any primary relation. Keep every
   valid unfavorable result and every invalid/inapplicable disposition.
   An invalid or incomplete trial cannot be counted, replaced, dropped or
   relabeled as successful coverage. Failure to complete the fixed panel
   leaves this proposed route incomplete under the existing failure rules.

## Requirements that remain unchanged

- All four participants, both original fusiform ROIs, all 24 development
  concept blocks, the 480/120/120 concept roles and exact image identities.
  All four people and both ROIs remain in every paired trial.
- P1/P2/P3/F1 identities, signed model order, frozen checkpoints, native
  voxel identities, B/C/D construction, common prediction banks, q32/N256,
  the 32-member anatomical bank, R_N, V_NC and ceiling floor0.10.
- The seven frozen primary endpoints and five directed edges; one changed
  measurement operation per registered edge. No composite or newly invented
  edge becomes a primary comparison.
- Existing training-only transformations and common hyperparameter selection,
  finite-input/moment requirements, every applicable variance requirement,
  strictly positive finite individual-block SST, and the adopted narrow
  uncertainty-block-only variance amendment. No numerical threshold is
  relaxed by this proposal.
- The complete within-pair F_raw and F_raw_normalized families, unchanged
  10,000 draws/seed1729, studentization, simultaneous bounds, practical
  margins, adequacy widths and undefined-draw refusal. These remain
  within-pair guarantees; fixed-panel completion adds no experiment-wide
  multiplicity guarantee across selected model relations.
- Original forecasts, native falsifiers, uncertainty/influence conditions
  and development-to-audit homologs. Family001 has no eligible H_BC/H_CD
  components. Beta primary comparisons remain evaluable where valid, but
  their H-dependent explanatory claims/forecasts remain inapplicable; the
  proposal does not repair, replace or waive them.
- At most one selected primary relation and two disclosed secondary checks.
  Correlation, CKA and ROI/semantic conditionality cannot rescue a failed
  primary claim or become independent candidate-ready outcomes.
- The original evidence gates before the single audit opening, frozen
  prediction bank and choices, no audit refit, no audit-driven selection,
  participant/block direction requirements, access disclosures and claim
  limits. Completing 20 trials alone never authorizes audit or a positive
  scientific conclusion.
- The 6,000 CPU-core-hour, 256 GPU-hour, 4-TiB and 240-hour episode limits,
  including the unchanged October13 18:29:09UTC deadline (1791916149).
  No new job, automatic retry or budget/deadline extension follows here.

## Benefit and scientific cost

The benefit is a reviewable, complete comparison of the already frozen model
pairs and isolated measurement operations, with the required random-model
falsifier and no fabricated trial counts. Existing real-data fitting and
endpoint work contribute directly to this fixed panel.

The cost is narrower search coverage: the study would not demonstrate two
adaptive cycles, systematic incumbent challenges or exhaustion of a 32–72
trial search. A result could describe the fixed registered comparisons and,
only after the unchanged audit gates pass, their transfer to new concepts in
the same four people. It could not claim robustness to untested measurement
operators, adaptive search completeness or new-participant replication.

If adopted, preserve the original policy/history and this decision as an
explicit amendment, record the current score-exposure disposition, and update
the operative completion rule before using it. If declined, the original
requirements remain in force and the current 20-trial roster must not be
reported as a completed adaptive search. No private score inspection is
needed to decide between these routes.
