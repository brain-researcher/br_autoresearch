## Recommended deterministic balance

Use exact role sizes as hard constraints. Freeze four equally weighted
families of concept-level covariates before assignment:

- Taxonomy: the membership indicators and explicit unmapped indicator above.
- Exemplars: original five/six status and qualified image count.
- Acquisition: for each participant, the fraction of retained concept
  presentations in each source session, and their mean and variance of
  normalized actual acquisition rank. Give each participant equal weight.
- Repetition lag: separately summarize within-session seconds and
  between-session days for each participant, retaining expected first-repeat
  missingness and unexplained unknowns. The source uses zero seconds as a
  between-session sentinel and zero days for within-session repeats; these
  are not zero physical delays. Do not combine them as one seconds variable
  or replace either with a retained-index gap. Transformations, scaling and
  missing-value conventions remain prospective pending numerical review;
  no balance matrix or optimizer is selected now.

For each nonconstant covariate, standardize by its population standard
deviation over the whole eligible universe. Minimize the average of the four
family losses. A family's loss is its mean covariate loss; a covariate loss is
`sum_r p_r * (mean_r - mean_all)^2 / variance_all`, where `p_r` is the fixed
role fraction. Within acquisition and lag families average participant losses
equally, then their fields equally. Constant fields contribute zero. All
transformations use design metadata only. Hard-count tolerance is zero;
covariate imbalance is a soft objective, with every residual reported and no
post-hoc rejection threshold or search for an attractive assignment.

Recommend a single reproducible start ordered by SHA-256 of a frozen
namespace and the canonical concept identifier; assign the first 480, next
120 and last 120 to the named roles. Then apply the best strictly improving
cross-role pair swap, breaking equal gains by the canonical pair of concept
IDs, until no improving swap remains. Describe the result as a deterministic
pairwise local optimum, not a globally optimal split. Freeze UTF-8 encoding,
identifier serialization, role order, the namespace
`ep17-source-roles-v1`, and the implemented numeric comparison precision before
execution. A resource stop produces an incomplete attempt, never a selected
partial assignment; do not restart with different seeds seeking better
scientific results.

Apply the same construction to six equal development folds, separately to 24
equal development blocks, and separately to twelve equal audit blocks, using
distinct frozen namespace suffixes `/outer-fold`, `/development-block`, and
`/audit-block`. Use only the parent role's metadata and its own normalization
for each construction. The development-block procedure must not receive fold
labels, impose fold balance, or derive blocks by subdividing folds. Distinct
namespaces and no fold input implement the declared construction boundary;
they are not a claim of probabilistic independence between deterministic
metadata-balanced partitions. All exact sizes remain mandatory.
