## Initial generation — exact prompt

```text
Use case: scientific-educational.
Create a new landscape scientific concept figure for EP20, white background, large navy sans-serif labels, muted teal/orange/purple, generous whitespace. Three panels A–C, journal graphical-abstract style, drawings rather than dense paragraphs.
Exact title: "When does a NeuroCam redesign help?"
A, "Field and device conditions": four clean small illustrations in two rows: a broad versus fine cortical-surface voltage field labelled "Spatial scale"; a slow versus rapid schematic waveform labelled "Temporal scale"; a pad/electrical-noise glyph labelled "Noise + impedance"; a switching response glyph labelled "Settling + crosstalk". No empirical axes, values or fitted measurements. All inputs concern virtual design conditions.
B, "Which design should help?": a large clean 2×2 table, not a free-form curved boundary. Columns labelled "No spatial benefit" and "Spatial benefit"; rows labelled "No time-focused benefit" at the TOP and "Time-focused benefit" at the BOTTOM. Exact cell mapping is crucial: TOP-LEFT "D0  Reference"; TOP-RIGHT "D2  Geometry"; BOTTOM-LEFT "D1  Scan + software"; BOTTOM-RIGHT "D3  Joint". Draw simple array/scan icons within each cell. Beneath the table: "Time-focused = scan + reconstruction". No claim that the temporal factor is hardware-only, no D4.
C, "Does the prediction survive?": a proposed array goes into two equally sized abstract boxes "Accepted model A" and "Accepted model B", leading to a neutral question "Same design preference?". Below two branches labelled "Agreement: test held-out conditions" and "Disagreement: measure the missing physics". No observed winners or fake results, no STOP sign. A single final line says "Rule failure: descriptive comparison only".
Only footer: "Virtual design study — no fabricated-device result".
Avoid imagined superiority, model-specific promotion after rule failure, accuracy bars, fabricated numbers, dense pass/fail tables, lock icons, 3D chips, skull art and repeated caveats. Preserve the exact table-cell mapping.
```
