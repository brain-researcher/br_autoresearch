# Research landscape note

2026-10-02 — [Scope/novelty review (consolidated)](paper_plan.md): Pad-size/noise and multiplexing trade-offs are established. The potential contribution is robustness across accepted models and an independently tested condition-to-design rule, or a useful measurement-identifiability boundary.

## What the NeuroCam paper already establishes

The published work reports a 4,096-channel flexible multiplexed array and
characterizes several scan settings, noise levels, gain, bandwidth, and
crosstalk. EP20 cannot claim the architecture or its trade-offs as new.

## The open gap

The unresolved question is whether a constrained change in pad geometry and
scan allocation adds robust field-reconstruction value beyond optimized
software, the hardware change alone, and simple resource-matched rules.

## Current contribution status

This is a proposed virtual comparison, not a finding. No reference model has
passed its held-aside checks, no candidate has been scored, and no design has
been recommended for fabrication.

## Design refinement — 2026-09-30

The proposed contribution is now expressed through coupled field/measurement/
reconstruction comparisons. Equal spectral-scale summaries can omit orientation
and phase, providing a concrete challenge to the proposed condition rule.
C-arm software/acquisition comparisons remain distinct from D-family bundled
contrasts. This is a refined hypothesis, not evidence of superiority or novelty.

## Concrete design contrasts — 2026-09-30

The starting catalogue now contrasts half-/quarter-area pads, paid-for revisit
or dwell allocation, and broad versus fine/rapid fields. Literature-context
controls prevent a proposal from being justified only by adversarial sub-pitch
truth. This makes the hypothesis testable, not a new result or novelty claim.

## Coupled electrical hypotheses — 2026-09-30

The proposal now tests contact-area scaling and persistent array/DAQ memory
instead of assigning independent favourable pad/scan penalties. Common-panel
probes make its conditional design-rule inputs operational. This is a more
specific virtual hypothesis, not evidence of novelty, superiority or fabrication
readiness; all six templates remain unfit and unaccepted.

## Compact choices — 2026-09-30

Source-depth/contact-gap sensitivities and short/long causal histories now make
the proposed comparison concrete; they are not new evidence or novelty claims.

## Anchor roles — 2026-09-30

The proposed reference must predict full-mode noise and distant coupling without
fitting them. This is a paper-consistency test, not new empirical evidence.

## Scientific framing — 2026-09-30

The paper opening now separates hardware value beyond software, predictive S/T
separation versus coupled rivals, and whether published measurements identify
a design preference. These are competing hypotheses, not novelty findings.
