# EP20 scope, novelty and clarity review — 2026-10-02

Question: do pad geometry and scan allocation recover cortical-surface voltage beyond optimized published hardware, and can measurable field/device conditions predict that advantage across accepted reference models?

## Closest work and the remaining contribution

[Optimal Electrode Size for Multi-Scale Extracellular-Potential Recording (2019)](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/neuroscience/articles/10.3389/fnins.2019.00385/full) already addresses electrode-size, spatial-averaging and noise trade-offs. [Multiplexed Surface Electrode Arrays Based on Metal Oxide Thin-Film Electronics](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10933637/) makes switching and multiplexing limitations central prior work. The [NeuroCam source paper](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.scib.2025.11.030) supplies the device anchors; generic small-pad or faster-scan benefits are not new.

The potentially useful contribution is a robust, resource-matched virtual comparison plus an independently tested condition-to-design rule. Equally plausible electronics models reversing the preferred design would instead identify a missing measurement, not an optimum.

## Decision and repair

Keep the scientific design. The policy already requires the held-out design-rule test for promotion. GOAL and paper-plan prose incorrectly allowed a rule-failing average winner to advance as a model-specific specification. That branch now remains a descriptive simulator-dependent result, with no promotion under the current rule. No gate was loosened or added.

The time-focused factor bundles scan and reconstruction software; it is not an isolated temporal-acquisition intervention. The figure wording now says so.

This is a virtual design/identifiability study, not fabricated-device validation or evidence that a pad layout improves neural inference in vivo. Source-to-surface and electrical uncertainties are substantive scientific limits. Candidate exploration remains within the existing broad legal space; this review does not substitute a tiny fixed menu.

No numerical margin, candidate family, resource limit, measurement requirement or execution authority changes. No simulation or device result was produced.
