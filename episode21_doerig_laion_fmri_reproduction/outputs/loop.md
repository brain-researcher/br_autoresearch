# Loop

EP21 remained at the contract-only stage after its 2026-09-28 initialization.
No empirical handoff has been provisioned, no analysis attempt has started,
and no process or scheduler job is active. If empirical work is separately
authorized, the next scientific milestone is `source_qualification`, not
neural scoring.

The fixed workflow is:

```text
source qualification
    -> pipeline conformance and protocol lock
    -> outcome-blind feature preparation
    -> separate Finding 1, Finding 2, and Finding 3 branches
    -> prespecified robustness and cross-modal extension branches
    -> integrated report
```

The three findings are sibling evaluations after their required feature
preparation; one finding does not choose the method or continuation of another.
Each unavailable branch is frozen as `not_evaluable` before neural scoring and
does not block an otherwise qualified branch.

There is no incumbent, challenger, outcome-adaptive successor, patience rule,
or candidate lock. A demonstrated technical defect may be repaired and replayed
at the smallest affected stage with the scientific configuration unchanged.
A consequential change after any neural score stops EP21 rather than opening a
new search inside it.

The current blockers are the absent role-filtered handoffs, unresolved release
and raw-image permissions, unauthenticated model assets and method details, and
the incomplete protocol lock. Contract authoring alone does not clear them.
