# Memory

EP21 was initialized on 2026-09-28 as a fixed, adapted transfer reproduction.
The scientist authorized contract authoring only; no empirical execution or
neural access has been authorized or recorded.

The durable scientific decisions at initialization are:

- EP21 tests three directional findings from Doerig et al. on regular
  LAION-fMRI images. Differences from the original NSD participants, stimuli,
  preprocessing, ROIs, metadata, and inference remain part of the estimand.
- The 4,712 regular subject-unique images per participant are reserved for
  frozen fitting. The 1,121 regular shared images are fixed retrospective
  evaluation evidence already exposed in the EP04 lineage.
- EP04 and EP21 are correlated analyses, not independent confirmations. Live
  EP04 outputs, rankings, runtime state, and legacy Scratch are forbidden EP21
  inputs.
- The entire 371-image OOD payload—raw images, derived features, neural
  responses, predictions, scores, and summaries—is closed. Only published
  category-level descriptions may be cited.
- Participants are the biological observations. A prespecified subclaim needs
  all five participants, at least four effects in the predicted direction, and
  corrected significance for at least three effects in that direction to be
  called `replicated`.
- The scientific result is an ordered vector of three finding verdicts, not an
  episode-wide scalar. Secondary robustness and cross-modal analyses cannot
  rescue a failed primary finding.

The source handoffs, release and DUA status, raw-image permission, model and
decoder assets, RCNN aggregation rule, paired-comparison inference, and other
protocol fields remain unresolved. `PROTOCOL_TABLE.yaml` is intentionally
unlocked. Every affected field must be resolved outcome-blind, or its branch
must be frozen as `not_evaluable`, before any neural score.
