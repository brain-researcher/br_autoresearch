# Verification

## 2026-09-28 initialization validation

- Episode structure: the Goal, data contract, fixed search policy, protocol
  table, input/output documentation, seven local status files, paper plan, and
  ASTRA experiment program are present.
- Scientific identity: the documents consistently define EP21 as a fixed,
  adapted, retrospective transfer reproduction. They do not describe an exact
  reproduction, adaptive search, independent EP04 confirmation, or OOD test.
- Evidence dependence: EP04 and EP21 are explicitly correlated through their
  shared LAION-fMRI source. The 1,121 regular shared images are labeled as
  previously exposed evaluation evidence.
- Contract boundary: the episode documents prohibit live EP04 outputs and the
  entire 371-image OOD payload, including raw images, derived features, neural
  responses, predictions, scores, and summaries. Published category-level
  descriptions are the only permitted OOD reference.
- Inference contract: participants are the biological observations; the
  five-participant eligibility requirement and the four-of-five direction plus
  three-of-five corrected-significance rule are explicit.
- Program structure: Findings 1–3, robustness analyses, sensitivities, and the
  cross-modal extension are separate ASTRA analyses. Every scored analysis
  requires the protocol lock, and the source paper's OOD Method 3 is absent
  from the executable program.
- Program validation: the ASTRA 0.0.14 schema and semantic validators passed
  with zero warnings at initialization.

## Open scientific and access gates

- No read-only, role-filtered EP21 empirical handoff has been provisioned.
- Release identity, DUA, Sherlock risk classification, joins, masks, features,
  split membership, code, weights, decoder dictionaries, and comparator
  coverage have not been qualified.
- The RCNN ten-instance aggregation, paired-comparison method, and remaining
  protocol-table fields have not been resolved and locked.
- Pipeline conformance has not been demonstrated on the required synthetic or
  permuted fixtures.
- No neural value, prediction, or model–brain score has been accessed or
  computed. No process or scheduler job has run for EP21.

These checks verify the design record, not empirical readiness or a scientific
result. Contract authoring did not authorize execution. The presence of an OOD
prohibition in the documents also does not substitute for qualifying the
physical role-filtered handoffs before use.
