wrong source and supplied the Wang et al. neural-code-conversion proposal. That
instruction authorizes correction of EP21's identity and local contracts. It
does not authorize empirical access, raw-stimulus opening, feature extraction,
model fitting, neural scoring, a local process, or a Slurm job.

The correction is scientifically clean because no EP21 neural value, derived
score, or result was accessed under the superseded design. The old draft and
figure have no active scientific role. A later empirical run still requires
source qualification, DUA and Sherlock risk clearance, role-filtered
handoffs, method conformance, a complete protocol lock, and explicit execution
authority.

another. EP21 may not use live EP04 outputs, candidate rankings, runtime state,
or legacy EP04 Scratch; EP21 artifacts may not score, rank, prune, promote, or
audit an EP04 candidate.

reservation of the 371-image OOD pool. Under the current contract, every OOD
raw image, feature, neural value, reconstruction, prediction, score, and
summary is closed to EP21. Enabling that branch requires an explicit
cross-episode allocation decision and a revised role-isolated contract before
any access.

No canonical Brain Researcher review, confirmation, reward, claim
registration, scientific acceptance, or Landscape transition is authorized.

role, model family, comparator, ROI, margin, inference family, or OOD boundary

neural score, such a change ends EP21 and requires a new episode.
