# Landscape

No scientific finding, claim, or shared Landscape transition is recorded for
EP21. Correcting the source proposal before empirical access changed the local
question, not the evidence landscape.

EP21 now asks whether Wang et al.'s content-loss converter preserves visual
information across people without shared training stimuli, first among five
LAION-fMRI participants and then, if independently provisioned, between LAION,
NSD, and THINGS sites. Regular shared LAION outcomes were previously exposed
in the EP04 lineage, so any LAION result is retrospective, correlated evidence.

The strongest possible result remains conditional on the sampled participants,
stimuli, scanner protocols, ROI proxies, and authenticated implementation. It
cannot establish population-wide neural-code equivalence, an OOD/artificial
stimulus result, site-invariant cortical identity, or brain-to-brain
communication.
