# EP21 scope, novelty and clarity review — 2026-10-02

Question: under this explicitly adapted data and measurement setting, do the prespecified neural-code conversion and reconstruction contrasts reproduce the Wang study?

## Closest work and contribution type

[Wang et al. (2025)](https://www.nature.com/articles/s43588-025-00826-5) and its [source implementation](https://github.com/KamitaniLab/InterSiteNeuralCodeConversion) are the direct target, not merely inspiration. Non-overlapping training stimuli and neural code conversion are source-paper ideas. EP21 should claim an adapted reproduction and a transparent boundary on transfer, not a new conversion method.

The fixed five-source, twenty-direction design does not supply twenty independent biological replicates. Training-package comparisons with different numbers and identities of stimuli cannot isolate loss objective alone; the optional matched-size control retains its separate role.

## Decision and repair

Keep the adapted-reproduction scope. Refresh only the paper-plan narrative;
prior history and current runtime remain untouched. Provisioned but sealed
neural artifacts are not scored results or permission to inspect them.
Execution contracts and separately recorded scientist authority govern
permitted stages; the execution ledger records actual attempts and next actions.
This review supplies no protocol lock, parent-job authority or empirical access.

The authorized provider-measurement exception, independent adapted visual-cortex mask and source-code version choices must remain visible adaptations, not be rewritten as exact replication. Where no equivalence margin exists, numerical resemblance is descriptive and comparability remains not evaluable under the current contract.

The manuscript's value depends on the reproduction evidence and the consequences of these adaptations, not a manufactured novelty claim or guaranteed standalone neuroscience paper. No current effect or successful replication is asserted.

No payload was opened and no runtime, code, lock, data role, threshold, job or authority was changed by this review. The existing empirical workflow remains independent of this narrative update.

A read-only Brain Researcher publication search for neural code conversion returned unrelated publications; it supplied no evidence for changing the design. The direct source paper and code, rather than that retrieval, support this positioning.
