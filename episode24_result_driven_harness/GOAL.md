# EP24 — Result-driven research harness

Created 2026-10-13. Standalone operational improvement episode; no new
neuroscience finding or canonical campaign is claimed.

## Question

Can a bounded, evidence-driven controller move an authorized research task to
its next interpretable result with fewer redundant approval requests, checks,
and unchanged waits, while preserving scientific and resource boundaries?

Progress means answering the next scientific comparison, including an
informative negative result or a valid scientific stop. Positive findings,
completed assets, job submissions, and documents are not interchangeable with
that progress. A genuine unresolved blocker can justify a handoff but does not
count as completion of the blocked experiment.

## Current work

The user authorized a new harness-improvement episode and analysis of the
complete recorded history of the original research thread. Analyze a fixed
byte-prefix snapshot of that session from its beginning through the captured
endpoint, then select and apply a small concrete harness improvement. The
coordinator owns transcript processing, shared records, and any changes outside
the files assigned to individual workers. Raw history remains read-only.

Use historical cases to distinguish wasted work from justified safeguards:
EP01 targeted reuse, EP04 valid stopping, EP08 a negative primary result, and
EP14 discriminating controls. These are cases to examine, not preassigned
behavioral verdicts. Separate exact recorded event counts from interpretations
of intent, redundancy, or causation; elapsed thread age is not unattended work.

## Candidate intervention and evaluation

Test the smallest change that addresses the observed failure: consume the
latest evidence, identify the next useful result, resolve its actual dependency
or resource obstacle, and execute within existing authority. Candidate changes
include reusing standing engineering authority, interpreting measured remaining
cost against the total budget, and carrying an authorized continuation across
resumes. Prefer a small patch and discriminative action traces to more generic
rules, checks, or a new benchmark framework.

Prospective cases may come from EP15, EP18, EP20, or EP21 only after their
current owners establish readiness and authority. Their contracts and owners
continue to govern all scientific runs. EP24 neither duplicates those jobs nor
opens their data. Compare recorded decision-to-action transitions, repeated
requests/checks without changed evidence, consumed resources, and completion of
the intended measured comparison. Report case differences and denominators;
unmatched before/after episodes do not identify a causal harness effect.

## Acceptance and limits

Acceptance requires an actually applied harness patch, at least one new
completed real-data result under the original episode's authority, and observed
process evidence showing how the patched behavior operated. Retrospective
analysis, created documents, simulated results, or a blocker record alone do
not validate the intervention. Report inconclusive or ineffective changes.

The initial analysis allocation is bounded at 1 CPU, 4 GiB, and 20 minutes in
Slurm. Engineering discretion operates within explicit remaining total caps;
it does not authorize unlimited compute, new scientific stopping rules,
eligibility changes, or holdout access. Subsequent consequential amendments
need existing delegated authority or an explicit decision. No external
communications, persistent services, or system-configuration changes belong
to this episode.
